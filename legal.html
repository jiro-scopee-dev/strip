Privacy policy

Your images

Images you choose are read by your browser and processed in memory on your device. They are never uploaded to a server. The cleaned files are written only where you send them: a folder you pick, individual downloads, or a ZIP your browser builds locally.

There is no account, no database, and no server-side copy. Closing the tab discards anything the page was holding.

What the page requests

The page is static HTML. It loads typefaces from Google Fonts and the ZIP library from cdnjs. Those providers receive the usual request data, such as your IP address and user agent, under their own privacy policies. Image data is never part of those requests.

Cookies and storage

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no local storage. The camera profile you select is not remembered between sessions.

Retention

Nothing is retained. Files exist only in the browser tab and wherever you choose to save them.
